### Story breakdown and planning

Use to clarify a Jira Story and plan its engineering tasks. Apply
[Staff's planning scope](../SKILL.md#autonomy) to every workflow call.

1. Run [jira-dev-task-from-story](../../jira-dev-task-from-story/SKILL.md) with
   the Story, repositories, and requested scope. It gathers evidence, grills
   requirements, selects planning tools, and drafts the breakdown.
2. Present its [review package](../../jira-dev-task-from-story/references/breakdown-format.md),
   including the proposed descriptions and plan comments. For a draft-only
   request, return the package and open questions here.
3. To publish, obtain approval of the ready tasks and their full content. Run
   [Jira task creation and updates](jira-task.md) with the approved subset,
   reviewed drafts, and recorded authorization. Keep other tasks as drafts.
